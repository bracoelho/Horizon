  <figcaption class="film-caption">One night, 9 September 2026. The first version was not flexible enough across different kinds of signal: one rule kept every Google News item off the shortlist whatever it scored, and of the four articles that cleared the floor that night, three were research papers and one was a news story - the score rewards stated evidence, which a headline cannot carry. Separately, a defect that night left 40 of the 226 items unjudged, that news story among them; the defect was fixed on 11 September. Version 2 groups what survives into five, named for what a story is about: security, reliability, governance and regulation, vendor dependency, and model capability. It changes the grouping, not the scoring, and it runs privately, as an experiment, reaching this site only after it is built.</figcaption>

<h2 class="section-label">The same night, in text</h2>

<p class="method-note">The film's nine stages, in order, with its own figures for that night. Five describe the whole field; four follow one article, &lsquo;Agents Trust Tools Too Much: Measuring Reliance on Unreliable Tools&rsquo;, as far as a single item can be followed. The film has no sound, and nothing in it is said only there.</p>

<ul class="sub-facts">
  <li><span class="k">Fetch</span><span class="v">The 30-hour window closes, and 226 items arrive from the feeds.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Gate</span><span class="v">A small model skims each opening; 132 pass, 40 of them unjudged.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Score</span><span class="v">A larger model reads up to 1,000 characters and scores each item.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Rank</span><span class="v">Compared in groups of seven, this article ranks third of 132.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Lead filter</span><span class="v">A rule on the source name holds back 52 aggregator items.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Shortlist</span><span class="v">Only the top ten go forward, and this article takes place three.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Defend</span><span class="v">The defender reads up to 6,000 characters: four published, six refused.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Score floor</span><span class="v">A published article needs 7.0. No item scored higher that night.</span></li>
  <li><span class="k">Enrich</span><span class="v">A larger model then enriches all four published articles.</span></li>
</ul>

<p class="method-note">(The pipeline on the method page has ten steps; the film shows nine. It never shows deduplicate or publish, and it gives the score floor a stage of its own.)</p>

<p class="method-note">Four articles cleared the floor that night, each scoring 7.0: three research papers and one news story. That news story was one of the 40 the gate never judged.</p>
